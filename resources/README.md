# Resources

We find these materials useful to learn more about open source, trust and safety, and related topics relevant to ROOST.

All resources are available free-of-charge unless noted otherwise.

## Open Source

- [The Open Source Way](https://www.theopensourceway.org/): detailed guidebook for building, growing, managing, and working effectively within an open source community; in general, the ROOST open source community follows this guide.

## Trust & Safety

- [<abbr title="Trust & Safety Professional Association">TSPA</abbr> Trust & Safety Curriculum](https://www.tspa.org/curriculum/ts-curriculum/): extensive documentation of what "Trust & Safety" is, including everything from its history and evolution to detailed break-downs of policy development and content moderation—and much more.

- [<abbr title="Trust & Safety Professional Association">TSPA</abbr> Trust & Safety Library](https://www.tspa.org/explore/trust-safety-library/): collection of articles and other resources helpful for trust and safety professionals, organized by category and topic.

- [Trust & Safety Teaching Consortium](https://trust-safety-teaching-consortium.github.io/TeachingTrustSafety/): curriculum covering trust and safety from a coalition of academic, industry and non-profit experts.
