# Resources

Learn more about open source, trust and safety, or related topics relevant to ROOST using these materials that we find useful.

## Open Source

- [The Open Source Way](https://www.theopensourceway.org/): a detailed guidebook for building, growing, managing, and working effectively within an open source community; in general, the ROOST open source community follows this guide.

## Trust & Safety

- [TSPA Trust & Safety Curriculum](https://www.tspa.org/curriculum/ts-curriculum/): extensive documentation of what "Trust & Safety" is, including everything from its history and evolution to detailed break-downs of policy development and content moderation—and much more.
